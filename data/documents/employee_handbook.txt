# NordicTech Employee Handbook

## Working Hours

NordicTech's standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00.

Employees should be available during their agreed working hours unless they are on approved leave or have an agreed flexible working arrangement.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely when their role and team arrangements allow it.

Remote work should be agreed with the employee's manager in advance.

Employees working remotely are responsible for having a suitable workspace and a reliable internet connection.

## Workplace Conduct

Employees are expected to communicate respectfully with colleagues and customers.

Company systems and information should be used responsibly and only for legitimate work-related purposes.

Employees should report suspected security incidents to the IT support team as soon as possible.
