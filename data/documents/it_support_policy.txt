# NordicTech IT Support Policy

## Reporting IT Issues

Employees should report IT problems through the company's internal support portal.

When submitting a support request, employees should provide a short description of the problem and include screenshots or error messages when available.

## Priority Levels

Critical issues affecting many employees should be marked as high priority.

Issues affecting only one employee and having a temporary workaround should normally be marked as normal priority.

## Password Problems

Employees who cannot access their account because of a forgotten password should use the company's password reset process.

Employees should never share their password with IT support staff.

## Hardware Problems

Hardware problems such as a damaged laptop, keyboard, mouse, or monitor should be reported through the IT support portal.

The IT support team may ask the employee to bring the equipment to the IT department for inspection.
