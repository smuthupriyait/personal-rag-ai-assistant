# NordicTech Leave Policy

## Annual Vacation

Employees are entitled to 25 paid vacation days per calendar year.

Vacation requests should normally be submitted at least two weeks before the planned vacation.

Employees should coordinate their vacation dates with their manager to ensure adequate team coverage.

## Sick Leave

If an employee is unable to work because of illness, they should notify their manager as soon as possible.

For longer periods of absence, employees should follow the company's absence reporting procedure.

## Parental Leave

Employees may apply for parental leave according to applicable Swedish laws and regulations.

Employees should inform their manager and HR as early as possible when planning parental leave.
